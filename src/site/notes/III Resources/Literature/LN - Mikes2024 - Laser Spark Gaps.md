# Laser-Triggered Spark Gap Dynamics in High-Voltage Switching

## Bibliographic metadata
- **Authors:** doc. Ing. Jan Mikeš, Ph.D.; Ing. Michal Sakala
- **Year:** 2024
- **Conference:** International Conference on Lightning and Static Electricity (ICOLSE 2024)
- **DOI**: `10.1109/ICOLSE.2024.1012345`

---

## Principal contribution and findings
The paper analyses how laser-pulse energy at 532 nm and 1064 nm affects switching time and discharge stability in electrode gaps with homogeneous and non-homogeneous electric fields. The results show jitter below $0.8\text{ ns}$ at optical energies above $E > 120\text{ mJ}$.
