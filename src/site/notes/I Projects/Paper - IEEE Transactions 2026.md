# Paper 1: Channel-State-Dependent Laser Triggering

## Manuscript objective

Demonstrate how measured laser-channel variables and the high-voltage working coefficient affect breakdown probability and the censored delay distribution, and whether channel-informed models improve held-out prediction over pulse energy alone.

The file name is retained for link stability; the journal and final manuscript title are selected after WP3 reveals the strongest defensible contribution.

## Evidence gate

The complete results narrative requires:

- frozen WP4 contrasts;
- calibrated timing and channel variables;
- QC-passed dataset with failed shots retained;
- held-out comparison for CL-03;
- independent repeat on another day or after an electrode-service boundary.

Methods, literature and figure shells should be written before this gate.
