# CL-06 - Reproducible optical and electrical stages

## Claim

Synchronized optical and electrical diagnostics identify a reproducible ordering of prebreakdown and breakdown stages, and a laser-to-HV delay scan reveals whether more than one channel-lifetime regime is required to explain the observations.

## Required evidence

- common time base and documented timing offsets;
- synchronized channel/emission, voltage and current records;
- physically motivated laser-to-HV delay range;
- stability of the inferred ordering across sessions;
- no stage label based solely on one ambiguous waveform feature.

## Decision rule

Support requires stable event ordering or a predeclared model comparison across an independent repeat. If the diagnostics cannot uniquely identify physical stages, report observable timing classes and their limits rather than assigning mechanism labels.
