# Thesis Structure & Chapter Outline

Doctoral dissertation: **Laser-Induced Plasma Structures for Protection and Switching in Electrical Power Engineering: Physical Dynamics and Techno-Economic Modeling**

*Author: Ing. Michal Sakala · Supervisor: doc. Ing. Jan Mikeš, Ph.D. · Czech Technical University in Prague, Faculty of Electrical Engineering*

---

## Monograph outline

### Chapter 1: Introduction & State of the Art

- **Objective:** Define the challenges of ultrafast switching and protection in modern power systems.
- **Topics:** Growth of renewable generation, limitations of mechanical and SF6 circuit breakers, and the principle of laser-triggered plasma initiation.
- **LaTeX file:** `chapters/ch01_introduction.tex`
- **Status:** Draft

### Chapter 2: Theoretical & Physical Foundations of Plasma Switching

- **Objective:** Develop the mathematical and physical description of optical breakdown and discharge evolution in gaseous dielectrics.
- **Topics:** Multiphoton and avalanche ionisation, electron and ion transport equations, Poisson's equation for space charge, thermal relaxation and shock-wave expansion.
- **LaTeX file:** `chapters/ch02_theory.tex`
- **Status:** Draft

### Chapter 3: Multiphysics Numerical Modeling (COMSOL)

- **Objective:** Formulate a 2D/3D axisymmetric model of a laser-triggered discharge.
- **Topics:** Gap geometry, boundary conditions, mesh refinement at the laser focus, plasma drift-diffusion equations and temperature-dependent reaction coefficients.
- **LaTeX file:** `chapters/ch03_modeling.tex`
- **Status:** In progress

### Chapter 4: Experimental Campaign, Diagnostics & Validation

- **Objective:** Present laboratory measurements and compare them with the numerical model.
- **Topics:** Nanosecond laser setup, high-speed optical (ICCD) and electrical diagnostics, statistical analysis of switching delay and jitter, and model validation.
- **LaTeX file:** `chapters/ch04_experiments.tex`
- **Status:** In progress

### Chapter 5: Techno-Economic Modeling & Power Engineering Applications

- **Objective:** Evaluate the integration of laser-triggered switching devices into transmission and distribution systems.
- **Topics:** Life-cycle cost analysis, contact-life extension, reduction of interruption duration through SAIDI/SAIFI modelling, and investment efficiency.
- **LaTeX file:** `chapters/ch05_technoeconomics.tex`
- **Status:** Planning

### Chapter 6: Conclusions, Scientific Contributions & Outlook

- **Objective:** Synthesise the original contributions, provide recommendations for practice and define future research directions.
- **LaTeX file:** `chapters/ch06_conclusions.tex`
- **Status:** Planning
